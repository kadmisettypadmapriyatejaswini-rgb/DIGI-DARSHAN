Somnath Temple

About Somnath Temple

The Somnath temple, located in Prabhas Patan near Veraval in Gujarat, is the first among the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Lord Shiva. This temple holds immense religious importance and has been reconstructed several times in history after repeated destruction.

The present structure was reconstructed in May 1951 under the leadership of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, in the Chaulukya style of architecture. It stands as a symbol of resilience and devotion.
