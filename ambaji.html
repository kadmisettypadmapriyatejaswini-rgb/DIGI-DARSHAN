Ambaji Temple

About Ambaji Temple

The Ambaji Temple, situated in the Danta Taluka of Banaskantha, Gujarat, is one of the most important Shakti Peethas in India. The temple is dedicated to Arasuri Amba, a form of Goddess Durga.

It is believed that the heart of Goddess Sati fell at this sacred site. The temple is visited by millions of devotees, especially during the Bhadarvi Purnima festival.

Location

Ambaji, Banaskantha, Gujarat

Deity

Arasuri Amba (Goddess Durga)

Festivals

Bhadarvi Purnima, Navratri

Significance

One of 51 Shakti Peethas

  • Afternoon Break: 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  • Evening Darshan: 5:00 PM - 9:30 PM

  • Sandhya Aarti: 7:30 PM

Guidelines

  • Please carry valid ID proof for verification.
  • Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the temple.
  • Follow temple rules and protocols.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled time.
