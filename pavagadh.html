Pavagadh Temple

About Pavagadh Temple

The Kalika Mata Temple at Pavagadh Hill, near Champaner in Gujarat, is a revered Shakti Peeth. It is dedicated to Goddess Kalika Mata and is situated at an altitude of around 800 meters.

The temple is an important pilgrimage center and part of the Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park, which is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Location

Pavagadh Hill, Champaner, Gujarat

Deity

Goddess Kalika Mata

Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Altitude

~800 meters above sea level

Timings

  • Morning Darshan: 6:30 AM - 1:00 PM
  • Afternoon Break: 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  • Evening Darshan: 5:00 PM - 9:30 PM
  • Mangla Aarti: 6:30 AM
  • Sandhya Aarti: 7:30 PM

Guidelines

  • Please carry valid ID proof for verification.
  • Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the temple.
  • Follow temple rules and protocols.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled time.
