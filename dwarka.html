Dwarka Temple

About Dwarka Temple

The Dwarkadhish Temple, located in Dwarka, Gujarat, is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites of India. It is dedicated to Lord Krishna and holds immense religious significance for devotees.

The present structure is believed to have been built around the 15th-16th century in the Hindu temple architectural style. It stands as a symbol of devotion, spirituality, and ancient Indian heritage.
